About

Research curiosity, engineered into working software.

Based in Andhra Pradesh, India, I have completed B.Tech and M.Tech studies in Computer Science and Engineering, specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science.

I enjoy turning technical ideas into clear, testable projects. My current work spans self-supervised image fusion, natural-language processing, machine-learning applications, and the architecture of AI-native knowledge platforms.

Reproducibility matters to me: documented assumptions, versioned configurations, automated checks, and honest limits are part of the result—not afterthoughts.
