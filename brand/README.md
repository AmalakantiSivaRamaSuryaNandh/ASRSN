# ASRSN shared identity

Use the same emblem across the website, portfolio, correspondence and work documents.

| Role | Color |
|---|---|
| Main structure | Emerald `#046A38` |
| Decorative lettering and accents | Royal gold `#D4AF37` |
| Secondary lettering | Platinum `#C7CDD4` |
| Dark background | Midnight navy `#071A2B` |

## Assets

- `../assets/brand/asrsn-logo.svg`: transparent editable vector artwork.
- `../assets/brand/asrsn-logo.png`: transparent PNG for general use.
- `../assets/brand/asrsn-signature.png`: compact PNG for email signatures.
- `../assets/brand/asrsn-social-card.png`: 1200 × 630 sharing image.
- `../assets/brand/favicon-32.png`: website browser icon.
- `../assets/brand/apple-touch-icon.png`: 180 × 180 device icon.
- `../assets/brand/brand.css`: shared color tokens.

Metallic gradients use lighter and darker shades of the base palette. This artwork is a vector recreation of the selected ASRSN logo, not a pixel-identical conversion of the original metallic PNG.

## Consistent use

Keep the logo proportions, original colors and transparent clear space. Do not stretch or crop the star, circle or letterforms. Give the emblem breathing room, approximately one tenth of its width. Use readable text alongside small logos; the detailed emblem is not intended to make all five letters readable at browser-icon size.

For websites, prefer SVG. For email, upload the PNG through the signature editor or use the deployed public PNG URL. Do not paste local filesystem paths into a sent signature. Use a short text signature for replies. Do not attach the logo automatically to every email.

Use the navy background for large presentation or profile applications. Use a plain light background for documents and correspondence. Apply the same logo to documents as an image in a header; the logo alone is not a handwritten or digital authorization signature.

General contact uses contact@asrsn.me. Research and business inquiries use their respective role mailboxes. The website branding does not merge inboxes or change mail configuration.
